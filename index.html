Our Story

Crafting Culinary
Excellence Since 2018

Cherryberry Foods and Catering was born from a deep passion for authentic Nigerian cuisine and the joy of bringing people together through exceptional food. Founded in Uyo, Akwa Ibom State, we have grown from a small family kitchen into one of the region's most trusted catering brands.

Our mission is simple: to deliver consistently outstanding food and service that leaves every guest reaching for seconds. From intimate family gatherings to large corporate banquets, we approach every event with the same dedication to quality and detail.

Our Mission

To serve exceptional food with genuine care, creating lasting memories at every event.

Our Vision

To become the most celebrated catering brand in Akwa Ibom State and beyond.

Frequently Asked
Questions

Everything you need to know about booking, orders, and our catering services.

You can place an order by calling or messaging us on WhatsApp at +234 901 025 0079, filling out the contact form on our website, or visiting any of our Uyo locations directly. We recommend booking at least 48 hours in advance for standard orders and 2 weeks ahead for large events.

Yes! Wedding catering is one of our signature specialties. We offer complete wedding food packages including appetizers, main courses, desserts, and serving staff. Our team works closely with you to design a custom menu that reflects the elegance of your special day.

For small orders, 24–48 hours notice is usually sufficient. For medium events (50–200 guests), we recommend booking 1–2 weeks ahead. For large events like weddings and corporate banquets, please book at least 3–4 weeks in advance to secure your date.

Absolutely. Our doorstep bulk delivery service covers large quantity food orders for events, institutions, and organizations. Food is transported in sealed, hygienic containers and delivered directly to your venue. Contact us for pricing on bulk orders of 50 portions and above.

Yes, absolutely! We pride ourselves on flexibility. You can mix and match dishes, request specific Nigerian recipes, accommodate dietary requirements, and create a fully personalized food experience. Our team will guide you during a free consultation.

Yes, we cater to events across Akwa Ibom State including Eket, Oron, Ikot Ekpene, and other major towns. For deliveries outside Uyo, additional logistics fees may apply depending on distance and order size. Contact us for a customized quote.

Yes! We provide trained, professional serving staff for events that require on-site food service. Our staff are courteous, neatly uniformed, and experienced in event catering. This service is available as an add-on to any of our catering packages.

We accept bank transfers, cash payments, and mobile payments via all major Nigerian bank apps (Opay, PalmPay, etc.). For large events, a 50% deposit is required to confirm your booking with the balance due 24 hours before the event date.
